About VSS Associates

Real land, bought right and handed over honestly.

We source, approve and develop land in and around Chennai — and we keep a simple promise on every layout: what is promised on paper is what you walk on.

The VSS way

Every plot we sell, we could stand on and defend.

VSS began with a frustration. Our founder spent his early career inside another real estate firm and kept seeing the same problem: paperwork that wasn't clear, and buyers left worried and confused by the very documents that were meant to protect them. He found that hard to watch — and eventually hard to work for.

So he left to build the firm he wished he'd worked at, and grew it from the bottom up, plot by plot. The way he runs it is what people mention first: he treats everyone with the same respect, has stayed modest and accessible despite the firm's success, and the office is said to feel like a family — with a freedom and flexibility rare in this business. We try to sell plots the same way: clearly, and without pressure.

What we hold to

Four commitments, kept on every layout.

These are operating principles, not slogans — each one is checkable on a VSS site.

01

Approvals before sales

Nothing is offered until the layout papers are in order. Approval documents are shared openly with every buyer.

02

The plan is the ground truth

Plot sizes, road widths and boundaries on the plan match the ground. If a survey disagrees, we fix the ground.

03

Honest pricing

One clear price per plot, stated upfront. No pressure escalation, no hidden charges introduced after booking.

04

Handover with papers

Registration support, patta transfer guidance and a defined handover checklist — the plot is yours in fact, not just in promise.

Design rationale — Screen 05 · About

  1. What is borrowed. Hosachiguru: the founder-story split (narrative beside a ground photo) and the “growth, verified” timeline that sells history instead of claiming it. G Square: the numbered commitments strip — four checkable operating principles rather than slogans.
  2. How it is adapted. The founder story is client-supplied (early career inside another real estate firm, unclear documentation, bottom-up growth, office-as-family culture) and is written in the firm's own voice; the pull-quote awaits the client's exact wording. The timeline is structured as Origins* / Founded* / Today / Next* — eras until real years and the founder's name arrive. Location is canon: Chennai (user-confirmed 2026-11-14). Proof band uses only real VSS site photographs; placeholder tiles stay in the carousel until real photos replace them.
  3. Why it improves on the previous proposal. The page sells honesty as the brand: every unconfirmed fact is asterisked, the strip counter (“4 layouts delivered”) matches the homepage, and the gallery keeps unverified shots out rather than inventing them.
  4. Client input still required. Founding year, founder's name, pull-quote wording, pipeline projects & target years, proof-band numbers (600 plots*, 120 acres*, 100 DTCP-approved*), and photos for the empty gallery tiles.
